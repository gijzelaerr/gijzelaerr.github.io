About.

One human, several time zones, a stubborn affection for radio waves.

Growing up in a music store, I developed an early love for both technology and sound. Now I'm a systems engineer at Spotify, which feels like a natural fit.

I have a PhD in radio astronomy and a master's degree in AI. Over the years I have worked in scientific computing, machine learning, industrial systems and large-scale infrastructure. For over a decade I was a freelance engineer under the name Pythonic. I built data reduction pipelines for radio telescopes like LOFAR and SKA, simulated the Dutch electricity grid as a testbed for reinforcement learning agents, and contributed to open source.

These days I work on Spotify's edge and service mesh infrastructure, keeping things running at scale.

I use AI as a daily collaborator, at work and in my side projects. The biggest of those right now are ra-ng for radio astronomy, a music workstation called Poing and a translation model for Namibian languages. They are all on the projects page.

Amsterdam is home, but I'm often in Windhoek and Cape Town. In my spare time I brew beer and make electronic music. I also DJ, and I keep a Discovery playlist on Spotify with whatever I come across, all genres, in no particular order.
