About.

One human, several time zones, a stubborn affection for radio waves.

Growing up in a music store, I developed an early love for both technology and sound. Now I'm a systems engineer at Spotify, which feels like a natural fit.

I have a PhD in radio astronomy and a master's degree in AI, with experience spanning scientific computing, machine learning, industrial systems, and large-scale infrastructure. For over a decade I worked as a freelance engineer through Pythonic, building data-reduction pipelines for radio telescopes like LOFAR and SKA, simulating the Dutch electricity grid as a testbed for reinforcement-learning agents, and contributing to open source.

These days I work on Spotify's edge and service-mesh infrastructure — keeping things running at scale. Current practice leans heavily on AI as a daily collaborator: using it to sharpen communication, accelerate troubleshooting, and work through complex problems more effectively.

Amsterdam is home, but I'm often in Windhoek and Cape Town. In my spare time I brew beer, make electronic music, and build things with AI.

Off-hours, there's a pair of decks. I DJ — less for the room, more for the one person who stops mid-sentence to ask what's playing. That same instinct feeds a running Discovery playlist: no genre, no commentary, no order worth defending. Tracks go in when they earn it. Listen if you're the curious kind.
