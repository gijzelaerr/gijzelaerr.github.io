A lab for software, signals & unlikely side-projects.

Run by Gijs Molenaar, systems engineer at Spotify, radio astronomer, occasional brewer and maker of electronic music. Est. 2008.

By day I work on Spotify's edge and service mesh infrastructure.

Before that I freelanced for ten years as Pythonic. I built data reduction pipelines for radio telescopes like LOFAR and SKA, simulated the Dutch electricity grid as a testbed for reinforcement learning agents, and maintained a handful of open source projects. I have a PhD in radio astronomy and a master's in artificial intelligence.

Right now I am working on ra-ng, which stands for radio astronomy, next generation. It is a Rust core with a Python command line that flags, calibrates and images MeerKAT data, and I want it to become a place where AI agents can do radio astronomy. For now it is a research demonstrator. Next to that there is s7commplus, the follow-up to python-snap7 for newer Siemens PLCs, Poing, a music workstation built around AI, and omalaka, open machine translation for Namibian languages. They are all on the projects page.

Amsterdam is home, but I'm often in Windhoek and Cape Town. In my spare time I brew beer, make electronic music and DJ.
